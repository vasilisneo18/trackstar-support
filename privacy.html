Information We Collect

  • Account & profile Your name, email address and the personal and fitness details you enter in your profile (such as age, height, weight and goals).
  • Training & nutrition Workout plans, logged sessions and sets, exercise comments, diet plans and meals, session bookings and gym check-ins.
  • Activities & location When you record a run, walk or ride, we collect your GPS route, distance, time, pace and elevation. When you check in at a gym, we use your location at that moment to confirm you are there. Location is only used while you record an activity or check in.
  • Groups & live sharing If you join or create a group, its members see your name, initials and today's activity stats (such as distance, top speed and vertical). If you switch on live sharing while recording an activity, your current position and speed are shared with the members of your groups only, every few seconds, until you finish recording. Live positions are deleted from our servers within 15 minutes. You can stop sharing or leave a group at any time.
  • Apple Health / Health Connect With your permission, the app reads your step count to show your daily and weekly steps, and saves the activities you record as workouts. Step data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers.
  • App usage The date you last used the app, your app language, and a device token so we can send you notifications.

How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to provide the Trackstar service: syncing your workouts, activities and diet across devices, generating personalised plans, sending notifications you have enabled, and enabling coach-athlete features. If you connect with a coach, your coach can see your workout plan, logged sessions and recorded activities, and your diet plan if you choose to share it.

We look at simple usage statistics — for example how many workouts an account has logged and when it was last active — to understand how Trackstar is used and to improve it. We do not show ads, and we do not sell your personal data. Health data from Apple Health or Health Connect is never used for advertising or shared with third parties.

We process your data to provide the service you signed up for, and use usage statistics based on our legitimate interest in improving the app.

Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

Your data is stored securely on our servers hosted on Railway (cloud infrastructure), and cached on your device so the app works offline.

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may delete your account and all associated data — including workouts, plans, diet, recorded activities and routes — at any time directly from the app (Settings → Close Account). Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Workouts you saved to Apple Health or Health Connect stay there until you remove them.

Children's Privacy

Trackstar is intended for people aged 16 and over. If you are younger, you may only use Trackstar with the consent of a parent or guardian where the law of your country requires it. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data without the required consent, contact us and we will delete it.

Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by emailing us or using the in-app account deletion feature. You can withdraw location and Apple Health / Health Connect permissions at any time in your device settings. If you are in the EU or UK, you also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
