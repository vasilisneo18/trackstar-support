FAQ

Questions, answered

Do my athletes have to pay?

No. Every athlete on your roster gets Athlete Gold free — it's included in your coach plan. They just download the app and join your team.

How do athletes join my roster?

You share a QR code or an invite link from the app. Your athlete installs Trackstar, scans or taps it, and they're on your roster in seconds.

What platforms is it on?

Trackstar is a native app on both iOS and Android, with push notifications, step tracking and offline support.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in. Pay monthly or annually (two months free on annual) and cancel anytime.

What if I have more than 50 athletes, or a whole gym?

We offer custom group access with volume pricing and Athlete Gold for every athlete. Get in touch and we'll sort you out.

How do I get started?

Pick a plan above and hit "Get started" — it opens a short email. We'll set up your coach account, usually the same day. No card required to begin.
